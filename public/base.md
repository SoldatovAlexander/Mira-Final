# Knowledge Base: AIR Mira

**What is Mira?**
Mira is the first AI recruiter in Russia that completely replaces a human recruiter for the sourcing and screening stages. It automates 100% of the routine work.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Speed:** Hiring is 5x faster.
- **Volume:** Can process 10,000+ candidates per month.
- **Availability:** Works 24/7 without breaks or holidays.
- **Satisfaction:** 4.9/5 average rating from 500+ companies.

**Core Features (The "What Mira Can Do" List):**
1. **Cold Search:** Automatically searches resume databases (HH, etc.) for candidates matching the job description.
2. **Screening:** Analyzes resumes, scores them against criteria.
3. **Interviews:** Conducts video/audio interviews with candidates, asks relevant questions.
4. **Analysis:** Analyzes Soft Skills, Hard Skills, and Emotions (facial expressions, tone of voice) during the interview.
5. **Offers:** Can automatically send offer letters to successful candidates.
6. **Analytics:** detailed hiring funnel, conversion rates, and economic efficiency reports.

**Benefits:**
- Reduces cost per hire by up to 50%.
- Eliminates human bias.
- Finds candidates that human recruiters might miss due to fatigue.

**Comparison:**
- **Human Recruiter:** Works 8 hours/day, screens ~20 resumes/day, subjective.
- **Mira:** Works 24 hours/day, screens unlimited resumes, objective data-driven analysis.
